Long-term calculators produce big numbers. A house worth ₹1 crore in 15 years, savings of ₹1 crore by retirement. But a rupee in 15 years buys less than a rupee today, so those numbers need translating before you compare them with anything you know.

## The conversion

Divide the future amount by inflation, compounded over the same years:

today's rupees = future amount ÷ (1 + inflation)^years

At 6% inflation, prices roughly multiply by 2.40 over 15 years. So **₹1 crore in 15 years is worth about ₹41.73 lakh today.**

The inflation rate you assume matters a lot over long periods:

- At 5%: about ₹48.10 lakh
- At 6%: about ₹41.73 lakh
- At 7%: about ₹36.24 lakh

## Real returns

The same idea applies to returns. If your investments earn 8% a year and prices rise 6%, your money grows in buying power by only about 1.9% a year (1.08 ÷ 1.06 − 1). A return that sounds healthy can be close to standing still.

## How the tools use it

Where a result is far in the future, our tools show it in today's rupees as well. The rent vs buy tool, for example, gives the gap between buying and renting after your chosen years, and the same gap in today's money, using the inflation rate in your assumptions.

Inflation is an assumption, not a fact we know. The default scenarios use 5%, 6% and 7% a year. Change it in the Assumptions panel if you expect something different.
