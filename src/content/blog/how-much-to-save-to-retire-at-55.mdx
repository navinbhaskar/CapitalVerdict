<InShort>

- To spend ₹50,000 a month in today's money from 55 to 85, at 6% inflation you need ₹2,14,594 a month at 55, rising every year after.
- That takes a pot of about **₹4.51 crore** at 55, at an illustrative 10% a year: about ₹1.05 crore in today's rupees.
- Starting at 30, that is a SIP of **₹40,265** a month for 25 years, or **₹15,770** to start if you raise it 10% each year.

</InShort>

Retiring at 55 means two stretches: 25 years of saving from 30, then 30 years of living on what you saved, to 85. Both are long enough for inflation and returns to matter more than anything else.

## Step 1: what you will spend

Say you want **₹50,000 a month** in today's money. At 6% inflation, prices are 4.3 times higher after 25 years, so at 55 you would need **₹2,14,594** a month. And because prices keep rising, that has to grow 6% every year until 85.

## Step 2: the pot you need at 55

Suppose the money stays invested and earns an illustrative **10% a year** before tax. A pot that can pay ₹2,14,594 a month at 55, rising 6% a year, for 30 years, after tax on the gains in each withdrawal, is about **₹4.51 crore**.

That sounds enormous. In today's rupees it is about **₹1.05 crore**: about 210 times the ₹50,000 a month you want to spend, in today's money.

## Step 3: the monthly saving

From 30 to 55, a SIP at the same 10% that leaves ₹4.51 crore after tax on selling needs:

- **₹40,265 a month**, the same every month, or
- **₹15,770 a month** to start, raised by 10% every year as your income grows.

The second is easier on day one but asks much more later: by 54 it is over ₹1.5 lakh a month.

At 8%, you need about ₹5.73 crore and ₹68,571 a month. At 12%, ₹3.66 crore and ₹24,044. Many people move to safer, lower-return investments as they near retirement, so the 8% row is worth taking seriously.

## What this leaves out

- **Your other savings.** Provident fund, NPS or a pension reduce how much the SIP has to do.
- **Big costs.** Health care, a home or a child's wedding need their own savings.
- **Bad timing.** A market fall just before or after 55 hurts more than the same fall at 35. Returns are averages, not promises.
