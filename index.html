How we work

  • Assumptions always visible

    Every number the answer depends on is listed beside it, with a source and a date. Change any of them and the result updates.

  • Conservative defaults

    When a value is uncertain, the default makes the costlier option work harder to win. Monthly savings, for example, are not assumed to earn interest.

  • Post-tax and inflation-aware

    Where a decision depends on returns or on money years from now, the tools account for tax on returns and for the time value of money, and say how.

  • No product recommendations

    We don’t name or recommend funds, policies, lenders or car models. The tools compare choices; picking a product is up to you.
