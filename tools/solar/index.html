    How this works

    Each month, the panels generate system size × generation per kW per day × 365 ÷ 12 units, less a little each year as they age. Your bill is worked out twice: on all the units you use, and on only the units you still buy from the grid, minus credit for any units you send to it. The difference is the saving. Because the bill uses the tariff's slabs, the units you save come off the most expensive slab first. Payback is the month when savings cover the price after subsidy.

    Questions

    Why are some fields empty?

    Subsidy, generation, panel loss and export credit depend on rules and data we have not verified yet, so we do not guess them. Enter the figures from your installer's quote and your bill.

    Why do I enter an average price per unit?

    Until the UP slab tariff is verified, the tool uses one price for every unit plus your fixed charge. Divide your energy charge and duty by the units on your bill to get it.

    What is the yearly return (IRR)?

    The interest rate at which the money you put into the system would grow to the same savings. Compare it with what the money would earn elsewhere, after tax.
