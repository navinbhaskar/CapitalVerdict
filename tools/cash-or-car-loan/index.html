How equity gains are taxed
  • Held 12 months or less: short-term, taxed at 20% of the gain.
  • Held longer: long-term, taxed at 12.5% of the gains above ₹1,25,000 in a year.
  • Plus 4% cess on the tax. PPF interest is tax-free; FD and RD interest is taxed every year at your slab rate.

    How this works

    The tool follows two buyers with the same money and the same car. The cash buyer pays the whole price today and then invests, every month, the EMI the other buyer pays. The loan buyer pays the down payment and the processing fee, invests the rest of the price, and pays the EMI. When the loan ends, both own the car and owe nothing, so the one with more invested is ahead.

    Questions

    Why does the cash buyer invest the EMI?

    So both buyers spend the same each month. The loan buyer's EMI goes to the bank; the cash buyer, who has no EMI, puts the same amount into savings. Without that, the comparison would favour whoever spends less.

    What is the loan's true cost?

    The yearly rate at which what you actually receive (the loan less the processing fee) equals the EMIs you pay. A fee makes it higher than the quoted rate. Your savings have to earn more than this, after tax, for the loan to come out ahead.

    Is the car's resale value included?

    It does not need to be. Both buyers own the same car, so its value at the end is the same on both sides and cancels out. To compare petrol, CNG and electric cars, use the car fuel tool.
