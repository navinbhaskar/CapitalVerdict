How this works

The tool works out your tax twice, once under each regime, with the official slabs, standard deduction, rebate and 4% cess for the tax year. Under the new regime most deductions are not allowed, so only the standard deduction and any deductions you enter as allowed there are taken off. Under the old regime, your deductions and exemptions are taken off as you enter them. Whichever leaves less tax is better for you.

Questions

What counts as deductions under the old regime?

Everything you would claim beyond the standard deduction: tax-saving investments and insurance, health insurance, home-loan interest, the HRA exemption and similar. Each has its own limit under the tax rules; enter the total you can actually claim.

What is marginal relief?

Under the new regime, income up to the rebate limit pays no tax. Just above it, the tax would jump, so marginal relief caps the tax at the income above the limit. The tool applies it, as the official calculation does.

What does the tool leave out?

Surcharge on taxable income above ₹50 lakh, the higher exemption limits for people aged 60 and over, capital gains and other income taxed at special rates, and rounding to the nearest ten rupees. For those, use the Income Tax Department's own calculator or ask a tax professional.
