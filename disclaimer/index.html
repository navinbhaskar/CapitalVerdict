Disclaimer

CapitalVerdict helps you think through a decision with your own numbers. It does not tell you what to do.

Education, not advice

Nothing on this site is investment, tax, legal or insurance advice. CapitalVerdict is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst, and is not registered with IRDAI. Speak to a qualified, registered professional before you act on a large decision.

Results are estimates

Every result depends on the assumptions shown next to it. Prices, rates, tariffs, subsidies and tax rules change, and the values here may be out of date by the time you read them. Returns, price rises and growth rates are assumptions you can edit, not predictions.

Market-linked returns can fall

Results for equity funds, SIPs, shares and property use a return you choose or an illustrative one. Past returns do not predict future ones, and the value of these investments can go down as well as up.

No product recommendations

CapitalVerdict does not recommend any product, fund, scheme, lender, insurer, car or installer.

Check before you act

Confirm prices, rates and rules with the seller, bank or authority concerned before you decide. If you find a mistake, please report it so it can be fixed for everyone.
